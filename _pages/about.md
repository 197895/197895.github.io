I am an undergraduate student majoring in **Artificial Intelligence** at the **School of Future Technology, South China University of Technology**. In **September 2027**, I will begin my Ph.D. studies at **Fudan University**, jointly with the **[Shanghai Innovation Institute](https://www.sii.edu.cn/yw/)**, advised by **[Prof. Siyu Zhu](https://sites.google.com/site/zhusiyucs/home)**.

My research interests center on **video generation** and **world models**.

I am open to **research collaborations** and **research internships**, especially in video generation and world models. Feel free to [get in touch](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).
